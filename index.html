About Me

Welcome. I'm Tucker Legerski — a storyteller drawn to the places where language meets lived experience. My work spans journalism, poetry, production, and the classroom, always guided by a commitment to honest craft and genuine curiosity.

Whether I'm reporting from the field, shaping a narrative for the screen, writing a poem, or working with students to find their own voices, I believe in the power of words to map the terrain of what matters.

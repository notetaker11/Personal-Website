About Me

Adventure best describes my career and life. I'm currently the Senior Producer at the Wisconsin-based Civic Media, helping folks make sense of the world with daily news and talk programming. I'm also the creator of the site Long Note, a publication about culture, work, and body policy. Before, I spent three years producing podcasts about business and tech for the media company WaitWhat.

Professionally, I've also been a public radio reporter, teacher, an anti-boredom machine at NYC children's hospitals, a Peace Corps volunteer out in Mongolia, and a pandemic delivery driver. I've written extensively about diabetes and living with crooked bones.

I live in Madison, Wisconsin with my spouse and daughter.

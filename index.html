About Me

I'm currently the Senior Producer at Civic Media, based in Wisconsin. Here, I help folks make sense of the world with daily news and talk programming. I also teach a journalism course at Madison College. Previously, I spent three years producing podcasts about business and tech for the media company WaitWhat.

I'm the creator of the blog Scroll Turner, an independent publication about books, class, longform journalism, culture, body/health politics, and local explorations.

Professionally, I've also been a public radio reporter, teacher, an anti-boredom machine at NYC children's hospitals, a Peace Corps volunteer in Mongolia, and a pandemic delivery driver. I've written extensively about diabetes and living with crooked bones.

I live in Madison, Wisconsin with my spouse and daughter.
